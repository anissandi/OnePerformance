5.	SCOPE
The Appraisal Management System (AMS) will provide a secure and user-friendly platform to manage employee performance evaluations across FMDQ. The system will support the full appraisal lifecycle for both confirmed and unconfirmed staff, from goal setting to final approval, while maintaining structured and traceable records of employee performance.
The system will support:
	Goal Setting Module for confirmed staff, enabling employees to define and submit – Common goals, Shared goals and Individual goals
	Mid-Year Check-In (H1 Review), allowing employees and supervisors to review progress against set goals and provide feedback.
	Full-Year Appraisal, enabling comprehensive performance evaluation and rating at the end of the appraisal cycle.
	Probationary Appraisal Module for unconfirmed staff to assess performance during the probation period.
	Employee self-assessment capabilities during appraisal stages.
	Supervisor performance evaluations and ratings, including first-level and second-level supervisor reviews.
	Automated appraisal workflows and approval processes, including the ability to return submissions for correction or further review.
	HRD Admin controls to activate or deactivate appraisal modules (Goal Setting, Mid-Year Check-In, Full-Year Appraisal, and Probationary Appraisal).
	Role-based dashboards for employees, supervisors, second-level supervisors, and HRD administrators.
	Performance history tracking and digital record keeping for employees across appraisal cycles.
	Reporting and analytics to enable HR and management monitor appraisal progress, completion rates, and performance trends.
The system will be integrated with the FMDQ intranet environment and employee directory to enable accurate employee identification, supervisor assignment, and workflow routing across the appraisal process.

8.	PROCESS OUTLINE
8.1.	Current Process Flow
The current employee appraisal process at FMDQ involves several manual and semi-manual steps:
	HRD communicates the appraisal timeline through email
	Employee prepare and document performance goals using spreadsheets
	Supervisors review employee performance through informal discussions and email exchanges
	Mid-year discussions between employees and their supervisor are conducted without a structured system for tracking outcomes
	At the end of the appraisal cycle, employees submit performance evaluations and supporting documentation manually
	Supervisors review and provide feedback before forwarding the appraisal for further approvals
	Final appraisal records are stored manually or across multiple files and email threads
Challenges include:
	Manual documentation of appraisal information
	Difficulty tracking approvals and revisions
	Lack of centralised performance records
	Limited audit trail for performance evaluations
8.2.	Future Process Flow (Proposed System)
Scenario 1: Goal Setting (Confirmed Staff)
	HRD Admin activates the Goal Setting Module
	Employees log into the system and define their Common Goals, Shared Goals and Individual Goals
	Employees submit their goals through the system
	The First-Level Supervisor reviews the goals and either approves them or returns them for revision
	Once approved, the goals are forwarded to the Second-Level Supervisor for final approval
	Approved goals are recorded in the system as the employee’s performance objectives for the appraisal cycle
Scenario 2: Mid-Year Check-In  (H1 Review)
	HRD Admin activates the Mid-Year Check-In Module
	Employees and their First-Level Supervisors review progress against the previously approved goals
	Employees provide updates on achievements, challenges and progress
	Supervisors provide feedback and document observations within the system
	The review may be submitted, approved or returned for revision where necessary
	The system records the mid-year feedback as part of the employee appraisal history
Scenario 3: Full-Year Appraisal
	HRD Admin activates the Full-Year Appraisal Module
	Employees and their First-Level Supervisors review progress against the previously approved goals
	The First-Level Supervisor reviews the self-assessment, evaluates performance, and assigns ratings
	The appraisal is forwarded to the Second-Level Supervisor for review and final validation
	Where necessary, the appraisal may be returned to the employee or supervisor for corrections
	Once approved, the appraisal is finalized and stored in the system as part of the employee’s performance record
Scenario 4: Probationary Appraisal (Unconfirmed Staff)
	HRD Admin activates the Probationary Appraisal Module
	Supervisors evaluate the performance of employees on probation
	The supervisor submits the probationary appraisal through the system
	The evaluation is reviewed and approved by the Second-Level Supervisor or designated authority
	The outcome is recorded to support decisions regarding employee confirmation or further review

12. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & INTEGRATION
The Appraisal Management System (AMS) will be implemented as an internal enterprise application integrated with the FMDQ intranet environment.
12.1 System Architecture Overview
The architecture will consist of the following layers:
1. Presentation Layer
User-facing interfaces used by different actors.
Users include:
	Employees
	First-Level Supervisors
	Second-Level Supervisors
	HRD
	System Administrators
Interfaces will be accessible via the FMDQ Intranet Portal.
2. Application Layer
This layer will handle the business logic of the system, including:
	Goal setting management (Common, Shared and Individual Goals)
	Mid-Year Check-In processing
	Full-Year Appraisal evaluation and ratings
	Probationary appraisal processing for unconfirmed staff
	Appraisal approval workflow across supervisors
	Submission reviews and revision handling
	Performance tracking and appraisal history management
	Reporting and analytics
The application will be hosted on FMDQ's internal application infrastructure.

3. Data Layer
The database will store all system records including:
	Employee appraisal records
	Goal setting submissions
	Mid-Year check-in feedback
	Full-year appraisal evaluations and ratings
	Probationary appraisal records
	Supervisor approvals and comments
	Audit Logs
	Reporting and analytics data
The database will be hosted on FMDQ's MSSQL database environment.

12.3 Data Flow Overview
The simplified flow will be:
1.	HRD Administrator activates the relevant appraisal module (Goal Setting, Mid-Year Check-In, Full-Year Appraisal or Probationary Appraisal)
2.	Employees access the system through the FMDQ Intranet and complete the required appraisal activity (e.g., goal setting, progress update or self-assessment
3.	Employee submissions are routed automatically to the First-Level Supervisor
4.	First-Level Supervisor review the submission, provides feedback and assigns rating where applicable, and either approves or returns the submission for revision
5.	Once approved, the submission is forwarded to the Second-Level Supervisor for final review and approval
6.	Second-Level Supervisor approves or returns the appraisal where further review or correction is required
7.	Approved appraisal records are stored in the system, and the data becomes available for reporting, analytics, and HR performance management purposes
